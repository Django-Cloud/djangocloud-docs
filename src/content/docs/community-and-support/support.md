**[Join the DjangoCloud Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/djangocloud/shared_invite/zt-4c3r2le5w-6BOCmVBJ3OykUwkCkUqlEg)**

## Asking a good question

You'll get an answer faster if you include:

* the **project name** (its slug) and the **release number**, for example `my-shop` v7
* what you ran: the command, or the push that triggered the deploy
* the **error or the last lines of the log**
* whether it worked before, and what changed

Never paste access keys, tokens or the contents of your environment variables.
